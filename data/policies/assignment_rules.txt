Assignment policy rules
1. A completed order delivered in more than 45 minutes qualifies for a full refund.
2. A cancelled order is not charged and is not eligible for a refund.
3. Refunds above INR 1,000 require approval from a human support lead.
4. A refund request must be made within 7 calendar days of order completion.
5. The normal delivery estimate is 30 minutes.
6. The delivery estimate during rain is 60 minutes.
7. The delivery fee is INR 25 for orders below INR 199.
8. Fraud cases must be escalated to the security team within 2 hours.
9. Premium customers have a response target of 1 hour.
10. Other customers have a response target of 24 hours.
11. A human handoff is required after 6 support steps.
